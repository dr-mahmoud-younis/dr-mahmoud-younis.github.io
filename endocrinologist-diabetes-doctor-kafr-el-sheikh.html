دكتور غدد صماء وسكر في كفر الشيخ – د. محمود يونس

د. محمود يونس استشاري الباطنة والغدد الصماء والسكر، وعضو الجمعية الأوروبية للغدد الصماء والجمعية الأمريكية للسكر. تقع العيادة في كفر الشيخ، برج الأطباء، أمام باب زيارة مركز الكبد.

  • أمراض الغدة الدرقية: قصور الغدة وفرط النشاط والعقد والتضخم والمتابعة بعد الجراحة

  • اضطرابات الغدة الكظرية والنخامية
  • السمنة ومقاومة الإنسولين واضطرابات الدهون
  • الباطنة العامة

Endocrinologist and diabetes doctor in Kafr El Sheikh – Dr. Mahmoud Younis

Dr. Mahmoud Younis is a consultant in internal medicine, endocrinology and diabetes, and a member of the European Society of Endocrinology and the American Diabetes Association. The clinic is in Kafr El Sheikh, in the Doctors' Tower, in front of the Liver Center visitors' gate.

What we treat

  • Thyroid disease: underactive and overactive thyroid, nodules, goitre and post-surgical follow-up
  • Type 1 and type 2 diabetes and follow-up of complications
  • Adrenal and pituitary disorders
  • Obesity, insulin resistance and lipid disorders
  • General internal medicine

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment with Dr. Mahmoud Younis?

Call one of the clinic numbers: 01010498414, 01123944884 or 0473234999.

Where is the clinic in Kafr El Sheikh?

Doctors' Tower, in front of the Liver Center visitors' gate, Kafr El Sheikh.

What should I bring to the visit?

Bring your previous tests and scans, a list of the medicines you take now, and any previous prescriptions or reports.

Which endocrine conditions does the clinic treat?

Thyroid disease, diabetes, adrenal and pituitary disorders, obesity, insulin resistance and lipid disorders.

Is there a follow-up visit?

A follow-up within one month is offered according to the clinic system; ask when you book.
